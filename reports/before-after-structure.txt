## Key Improvements

### 1. Service Organization
- **Before**: Mixed backend/frontend in root directories
- **After**: Clear separation with `apps/` and `services/` structure
- **Benefit**: Easier navigation, service-specific configurations

### 2. Deployment Standardization  
- **Before**: Raw K8s manifests, scattered configs
- **After**: Helm charts with environment-specific values
- **Benefit**: Consistent deployments, environment management

### 3. CI/CD Optimization
- **Before**: 27 workflows with significant duplication
- **After**: 12 consolidated workflows with reusable actions
- **Benefit**: 55% reduction in maintenance, consistent patterns

### 4. Container Security
- **Before**: Single Dockerfile, missing .dockerignore, no HEALTHCHECK
- **After**: Multi-stage builds, comprehensive .dockerignore, health checks
- **Benefit**: Smaller images, better security, improved orchestration

### 5. Documentation Structure
- **Before**: Scattered docs across multiple locations
- **After**: Organized by purpose (architecture, runbooks, API, development)
- **Benefit**: Improved discoverability, better onboarding

### 6. Infrastructure Organization
- **Before**: Mixed terraform configurations
- **After**: Environment-specific with reusable modules
- **Benefit**: DRY principles, easier environment management
