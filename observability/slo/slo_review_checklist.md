# SLO Review Checklist

- [ ] Are SLIs clearly defined and measurable?
- [ ] Are SLOs tied to business tiers?
- [ ] Is error budget tracked and visualized?
- [ ] Are burn rate alerts configured?
- [ ] Are dashboards version-controlled?
- [ ] Are SLOs published in the service catalog?
- [ ] Is SLO review part of engineering retrospectives?
- [ ] Are compliance requirements met (SOC2, HIPAA, ISO)?
